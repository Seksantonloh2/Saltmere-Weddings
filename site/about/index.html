Our story

One planner, start to finish.

Ellie Hart started Saltmere in 2018 after eight years running events for hotels. She saw couples stressed by planning that should feel exciting, and built a studio around one idea: one planner, start to finish.

Today the studio is Ellie, coordinator Jade and stylist Sophie, plus a trusted circle of freelance stylists. We keep the team small on purpose, so the person you meet on your first call is the person beside you on the day.
