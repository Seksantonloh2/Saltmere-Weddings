FAQ

Good questions to ask.

How far ahead should we book?

12 to 18 months for full planning; 3 to 6 months for on-the-day coordination.

Do you travel?

Yes, across South East Queensland. Travel outside Brisbane is quoted upfront.

How do payments work?

A 30% deposit secures your date; the balance is split into instalments up to four weeks before the wedding.

Can you work with suppliers we’ve already booked?

Of course. We’ll contact them and fold them into the plan.

What happens if it rains?

Every wedding has a written wet-weather plan, agreed well before the day.
